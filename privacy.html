Nocturne privacy policy

LumioLab · Effective 4 October 2026

Nocturne is a black screen overlay: it covers the screen with black so the app underneath keeps running while the screen looks off. This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone. The short version: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

No data collection

Nocturne has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device. LumioLab receives no information about you or how you use the app.

Settings

Your choices (clock face, colours, fonts and so on) are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Notification access (optional)

If you turn on notification access, Nocturne reads only whether media is playing, and its title and artist, so it can show the song on the clock and end the blackout when playback stops. It never reads the text of messages or notifications. This information is used on your phone, in the moment, to draw the screen. It is not stored, logged or shared.

Children

Nocturne is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
